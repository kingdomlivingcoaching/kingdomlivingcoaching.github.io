The why

I didn't have one. I want to be one.

My mom has told me more than once — she would have given anything, paid anything, for me to have had a godly man walking with me growing up. Someone showing me, on purpose and up close, how to actually be a man. Not just telling me. Showing me.

I didn't have that. I found a lot of the pitfalls the hard way instead. And I've spent years since then learning what I wish someone had handed me at twelve years old — how to walk in who I actually am, how to be disciplined in my body and my mind and my walk with Jesus, how to lead, how to treat people, how to build friendships that make me better instead of worse.

"This isn't a program I'm running at arm's length. I'll be vulnerable and honest about my own story, because that's where men actually grow — not in the polished version, in the real one."

Now I get to be that man for someone else's son. Two hours a week, one night, for as long as they'll have me. We'll sweat together first, then we'll sit down and talk about life — real questions, real struggles, real Scripture — and I'll be in it with them, not above it.

— Braxton Bonds, Founder
Certified Christian Life Coach · M.A. in Christian Leadership
How a night runs

Two hours. Two halves. One purpose.

Hour One — The Body

Train.

A workout, a run, or a sport — hard enough to matter, built for where they're at. Discipline in the body is where discipline everywhere else starts to take root.

  • Strength and conditioning work scaled to age and ability
  • Competition, sport, and team-based effort
  • Learning to push past "I don't feel like it"
Hour Two — The Soul

Talk.

We sit down, still breathing hard, and get into it — Scripture, real life, real questions. No performance. Just men figuring out how to follow Jesus in every room they walk into.

  • Teaching on identity, character, and following Jesus daily
  • Open floor for questions — nothing is off limits
  • Walking through actual life, actual struggles, together

Not winging it

A curriculum I built for this, year by year.

This isn't a guy showing up each week hoping he has something good to say. Every session follows a curriculum I wrote specifically for this age group — a real arc, building one year on the next, so nothing gets covered once and forgotten.

Each week pairs a spiritual discipline or character theme with time to actually practice it, not just hear about it — because kids can sit through a lesson on prayer and still not know how to pray on their own.

Accountability

Nobody navigates this alone — including you as the parent.

This group becomes an accountability structure, not just a class. These guys will get to know each other's real lives — the stuff going on at home, at school, in their own heads — and learn to actually show up for each other.

You'll hear from me directly on how your son is doing, not just get a monthly newsletter. I'm walking with him, and I want to walk with you too.

What that looks like weekly
  1. Every guy gets seen and known — no one blends into the back row.
  2. Real talk about what's actually happening in their lives, not just theory.
  3. Guys checking in on guys — the beginning of real brotherhood.
  4. Straight communication with parents on how their son is doing.
